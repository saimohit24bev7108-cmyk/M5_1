## Brand & Style

This design system merges the intellectual gravity of mid-century academic monographs with the razor-sharp precision of high-performance quantum computing simulators. It rejects the clichés of dark-mode neon cyberpunk, choosing instead a warm, tactile, newsprint-and-ink materiality. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Literary Rigor & Scientific Authority**: Deep charcoal ink pressed into warm ivory substrate, evocative of archival physics journals, physical lab notebooks, and scholarly monographs.
- **Architectural Minimalism**: Hairline grid dividers, monospaced computational margins, and clean mathematical notations that treat data as primary typography.
- **Controlled Kinetic Energy**: Precise terracotta/rust highlights used strictly for critical anomalies, state vectors, active execution gates, and primary catalysts.
- **Tactile Editorial Layouts**: Asymmetrical editorial columns paired with structured tabular instrument racks, treating quantum states and mathematical formulas with the same elegance as fine literary prose.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in classical physical mediums—paper, mineral ink, and drafting rust:
- **Backgrounds**: Layered ivory tones (`#FBF9F5` base canvas, `#F4EFEB` container background) invoke heavy-stock archival paper without glare.
- **Ink Tiers**: Rather than artificial absolute black, text and structural elements utilize deep carbon `#1A1A18` for primary figures and `#2C2C28` for body text, yielding high contrast without eye strain.
- **Terracotta / Rust**: Reserved for operational focus—running simulations, collapsed wavefunctions, probability amplitudes exceeding thresholds, and key call-to-actions (`#B44426` resting, `#C84B31` hover/active).
- **Drafting Slates**: Subtle technical blues and grays (`#748899`, `#C4CAD0`, `#E2DDD5`) construct Cartesian coordinate grids, Bloch sphere projections, and hairline rules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy operates through a deliberate three-tier system:
1. **Newsreader**: Brings classical editorial stature, literary pacing, and intellectual warmth to primary titles, section overviews, and executive abstracts. Headings should lean on natural italic variants for quantum variable names and mathematical theorems.
2. **Inter**: Provides high-legibility, neutral mechanics for configuration forms, secondary descriptions, parameter control labels, and general reading content.
3. **JetBrains Mono**: Serves as the computational spine. Deployed for Dirac bra-ket notations (`|ψ⟩`), matrix arrays, QASM code, gate parameters, timestamps, telemetry badges, and data grids.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout is structured around an editorial broadsheet discipline rather than standard dashboard card sprawl:
- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column dynamic grid on desktop (1280px+), collapsing to 6-column on tablet (768px-1279px) and single-column on mobile (<768px).
- **Asymmetric Composition**: Analytical dashboards pair a dense 4-column technical parameter sidebar (monospaced metrics, circuit assembly) with an expansive 8-column canvas (eigenstate graphs, probability density plots).
- **Separation by Rules**: Vertical and horizontal technical borders (`1px solid #E2DDD5`) establish structure rather than heavy background fills or elevation cards.
- **Rhythm**: Element padding adheres to standard increments (`0.25rem` micro-steps to `2.5rem` narrative chapter breaks) to maintain the cadence of a typeset technical journal.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects dropped shadows in favor of flat editorial materiality, subtle surface differentiation, and hairline boundaries:
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines**: Divisions occur via `1px` lines tinted with `#E2DDD5` or `#C4CAD0`. No blur shadows are permitted on standard cards or computational panels.
- **Tonal Stepping**: Depth is expressed through substrate value rather than height. The default canvas rests at `#FBF9F5`. Embedded circuit playgrounds, code consoles, and inspector panels shift to `#F4EFEB`.
- **Floating Overlays & Modals**: When an inspector or tooltip must float above the canvas, apply a crisp border (`1px solid #1A1A18`) backed by a minimal planar shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(26, 26, 24, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, slightly softened architectural geometry (Level `1` - Soft):
- **Base Geometry**: Interactive buttons, input fields, badges, and panel corners feature a minute `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, preserving the feel of precision cut paper and machined scientific equipment.
- **Badges and Pills**: Status badges and state chips remain strictly rectangular or softly clipped (`4px`), avoiding soft circular pills which clash with scientific precision.
- **Connectors and Circuits**: Circuit traces and quantum wire diagrams employ strictly orthogonal paths with 90-degree angles and sharp or `2px` micro-radii intersections.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid carbon ink background (`#1A1A18`), warm ivory label (`#FBF9F5`), `4px` border radius, `0.75rem` vertical / `1.25rem` horizontal padding. Font: `Inter` 500 weight or `JetBrains Mono`. Hover transitions to rust accent (`#B44426`).
- **Secondary / Outlined**: Transparent background, `1px solid #1A1A18`, ink label. Hover state adopts `#F4EFEB` background fill with subtle terracotta border.
- **Tertiary / Inline**: Unbordered, text-only with a subtle bottom hairline rule (`1px solid #C4CAD0`) that turns `#B44426` on hover.

### Badges & Simulation Status
- **Simulation Badge**: All-caps `JetBrains Mono` at `0.6875rem` with `+0.08em` tracking. Encased in a `1px` border with light neutral fill (`#F4EFEB`).
- **State Indicators**: Running/Active simulations feature a small square terracotta indicator (`#B44426`) with a gentle, non-glowing blink/pulse; Coherent/Idle states utilize `#748899`.

### Technical Input Fields & Knobs
- **Inputs & Sliders**: Background is clean paper (`#FBF9F5`) wrapped with `1px solid #C4CAD0`. On focus, border sharpens to `#1A1A18` with zero outer glow. Label set in uppercase monospaced text above the input.
- **Numerical Step Controls**: Monospaced values centered between hairline `+` and `-` increments, designed like optical bench calipers.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Flat ivory tiles (`#FBF9F5` or `#F4EFEB`) bounded by `1px solid #E2DDD5`. No drop shadows.
- Header bars feature an editorial label on the left (e.g., `FIG 04. STATE VECTOR DISPERSION`) and monospaced telemetry parameters on the right (e.g., `N=4096 SHOTS`).

### Quantum Circuits & State Registers
- Horizontal register wires drawn as crisp `1px #C4CAD0` rules over `#F4EFEB`.
- Quantum gates (H, X, CNOT) rendered as crisp ivory squares bordered with `#1A1A18` containing bold serif or mono notation. Selected gates illuminate with a `#B44426` border.

### Tables & Data Grids
- Full-bleed bordered tables with thin dividers (`#E2DDD5`). 
- Table headers typeset in `Newsreader` italic or `JetBrains Mono` uppercase with muted ink tone (`#6E6D68`). Tabular numeric data right-aligned using tabular lining figures.
